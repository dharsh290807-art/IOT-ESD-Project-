Wiring

PartESP32 pin
MPU6050 + OLED SDA / SCLD21 / D22 (3V3, GND)
DS18B20 DQD4 (3V3, GND)
Green / Yellow / Red LEDD25 / D26 / D27 via 220 Ω
BuzzerD14 via 220 Ω
Push buttonD13 to GND

Energy saving

  • 80 MHz CPU, WiFi/BT off
  • Burst sampling then 1 s light sleep
  • OLED off in Normal after 10 s
  • DS18B20 at 10-bit resolution
  • On-device 3-4-3 neural network, no cloud
